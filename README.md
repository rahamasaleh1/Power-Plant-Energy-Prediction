This project was the final assignment for Machine Learning Foundations for Product Managers, part of Duke University's AI Product Management Specialisation. The course is about understanding machine learning well enough to lead AI products, and this was the chance to put it into practice. I wanted to see how much a power plant's output really depends on the weather around it. Using readings from four environmental sensors (temperature, pressure, humidity and exhaust vacuum), I trained a Random Forest model to predict the plant's energy output. It explains 96% of the variation in output with an average error of just 0.5%, which shows how much you can learn from a handful of well chosen measurements.

## Data source

Pınar Tüfekci, *Prediction of full load electrical power output of a base load operated combined cycle power plant using machine learning methods*, International Journal of Electrical Power & Energy Systems, Volume 60, September 2014, Pages 126 to 140.
